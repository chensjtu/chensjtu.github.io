# Personal Page for Chen Yang ([chensjtu.github.io](https://chensjtu.github.io/))

This repository hosts Chen Yang's personal academic homepage through GitHub Pages.

Chen Yang is a Senior Researcher at HUAWEI. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2025, supervised by Prof. [Wei Shen](https://shenwei1231.github.io/), and received his B.S. degree from SJTU in 2019. His research focuses on world models for embodied AI, pretrained 3D generation models, and 3D reconstruction.
