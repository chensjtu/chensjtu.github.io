Chen Yang (杨 辰)

About:
I am currently a Principal Engineer at HUAWEI, working on research and engineering for world models and 3D intelligence in close collaboration with Dr. Jiemin Fang and Dr. Qi Tian. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2025, supervised by Prof. Wei Shen, and completed my bachelor's degree at SJTU in 2019. In 2023, I received the MICCAI Young Scientist Award for LerPlane, my work on fast 4D reconstruction of deformable tissues. I have published 20+ papers at top international AI conferences and journals, with over 1,500 citations on Google Scholar.

Research Vision:
My research centers on building foundation world models for embodied intelligence. I develop scalable models that can generate, reconstruct, and simulate complex 3D environments, with the goal of enabling intelligent agents to understand, imagine, and interact with the physical world. My work spans pretrained 3D generative models, high-quality 3D reconstruction, and infinite world generation, forming a unified path toward general-purpose spatial intelligence. Most representative projects publish code and detailed artifacts to support reproducible evaluation and fair comparison; the evidence is collected in my open research record. I believe world models will become a fundamental infrastructure for embodied AI and a critical step toward AGI.
